The rise and growth of e-commerce has impacted the society and the way that people shop. The introduction of plartforms like Amazon and Ebay have made shopping such easier by allowing them to buy products from home without having to physically visit the shop. This has saved people a lot of time and made products accessible especially those that stay in rural areas who have difficulty in travelling. We also discored that e-commerce has created job opportuities in warehouse, delivery services and customer services.
Another important aspect of e-commerce is that it has changed how businesses operate and compete with each other. Online shopping allows smaller businesses to reach customers from diferent locations without needing to open physical shops. This gives smaller companies more oppotunities to grow and compete with larger companies. E-commerce also allows businesses to advertise their products through social media and online platforms making it easier to attract new customers. 
However, i discovered that the rise of e-commerce has also negatively impacted the society. Traditional shops have seen a reduction on cutomers since people now prefer shopping online. It has also reduced some face to face interactions between customers and shop workers. Another concern has been online safety as customers always have to share personal and payment information when having to purchase items.
Another social impact is that not every person has equal access to e-commerce. People without reliable internet access or skills on how to use to shopping services can have dificulty. This creates a gap between people who are comfotable using the shopping service and the ones who are not.
I also discovered that business also face challenges, such as strong competition, delivery costs and the need to protect customer information. Another issue is that customers cannot physically see or try products before purchasing them, which can sometimes lead to dissapointment if the product doesn't meet their ecpectations. Returning products can also be inconvenent for customers and expensive for businesses.
Environmental concerns have also emerged. While online shopping an reduce the number of individual car trips to stores, it has led to a larger increase in packaging waste and carbon emissions from delivery vehicles. The expectations of faster and free delivery has put pressure on logistics systems and sometimes results in inefficient practices.
Overall e-commerce has made shopping easier, more convenient and more accessible for many people. It has created new economic opportunities and allowed small business to grow in ways that were previously impossible. At the same time, it has changed shopping habits, affected traditional employment, reduced certain forms of interactions, raised privacy  and security issues and contributed to environmental challenges. Society continues to adapt to these changes and long-term e-commerce depend on how well governments, businesses and individuals manage both its benefits and its challenges.
