#Introduction
We investigated the rise of e-commerce (ebay/amazon evolution). We analysed the thesis statement through two viewpoints. These viewpoints were from a business viewpoint and a societal viewpoint. As a group we covered both the benefits and issues that arised from the growth of these companies and compiled our evidence within this project to create our overview of the topic. 
The rise of e-commerce dates back to 1979 when Michael Aldrich first introduced online shopping to the world market by using a modified domestic television that was interconnected to a transaction processing computer through a telephone line. This complex set up sparked the beginning of ecommerce and inspired both eBay and amazon to compete within the multi billion dollar industry that exists today.
