#2 Historical Development (350 words)
E-Commerce has been one of the defining developments of the modern world. In 2025 alone it accounted for a fifth of worldwide retail sales and was valued at over 4 trillion in estimated revenue in 2025. More than the entire economic output of Turkey and Spain combined. But E-Commerce did not always enjoy its large market share. E-Commerce had a long, difficult and challenging development. It has its origins in the United States during the significant events of 1969 in Columbus, Ohio with the opening of CompuServe the nation and worlds first non human operated commerce operation. Due to the lack of internet this early version had data traveling through phone lines. This company laid the foundations for later "true" E-Commerce such as the Boston Computer Exchange (BCE) which is recognised as the first true E-Commerce company which was used to buy and sell used computers, operating before the innovation of the World Wide Web by utilising dial up technology.

While not a glamorous an important part of development on any emerging innovative technology, political recognition is massively important for something to be regulated and monitored to be widely accepted into existing supply chains.  Laws to be put in place to have consumer/investor confidence such as closing legal loopholes and frameworks to be established to regulate and control the emerging industry. One of the most significant political developments for E-Commerce was in 1984 when the state of California had a state assembly on electronic commerce. Testifying were many company's which wished to establish common laws such as CompuServe. On the other hand of the biggest enablers' of widespread E-Commerce is the development of the World Wide Web. Previously customers were reliant on slow and inefficient dial up internet speeds. The advancement of the WWW allowed people to more quickly and easily access websites, and as speeds got faster and faster E-Commerce was able to establish itself as a major pillar of modern logistics. Today E-Commerce has cemented itself as a major part of most people's lives in most countries, rarely would anyone's first choice to be to check in store for desired items and company's such as Amazon are valued in the Trillions, well above countries such as Ireland
