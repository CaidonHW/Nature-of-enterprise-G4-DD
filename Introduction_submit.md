We investigated the rise of e-commerce (eBay/Amazon evolution).This case study argues through the following thesis "the rise of e-commerce has made shopping easier and more accessible but also changed peoples habits and employment trends whilst effecting social interactions". We analysed the thesis statement through two viewpoints.As a group we covered both the benefits and issues that arose from the growth of these companies and compiled our evidence within this project to create our case study overview. We chose to analyse our thesis statement from a business and societal viewpoint as this was most relevant to our case study. Within the business viewpoint we researched the reasons the two companies thrived and some of their implemented business strategy techniques. From a societal perspective we covered the effect it has had on social interactions, employment and the harms on the environment.
Over the past decades electronic commerce has shifted from an interesting novelty invention into a worldwide phenomenon that has revolutionised how consumers shop on a regular basis. It has shifted through many eras of discs, to manufacturing goods, hardware, electronic appliances and even today groceries. E-commerce is now responsible for approximately 20% of worldwide purchases (according to emarketer 2025). The industry has continued to expand each year with a wider internet access growing and developments within the industry itself such as more secure payment systems. It has supported a whole new employment industry from warehouse workers, delivery drivers and pilots to a wide range of online jobs. These online jobs such as web designer, drop shipper and cyber security have become the centre of news headlines over the last decade as society becomes more dependant on the industry of e-commerce.
The rise of e-commerce dates back to 1979 when Michael Aldrich first introduced online shopping to the world market by using a modified domestic television that was interconnected to a transaction processing computer through a telephone line. This complex set up sparked the beginning of ecommerce and inspired both eBay and amazon to compete within the multi billion dollar industry that exists today. In terms of eBay and Amazon both companies were formed in 1995 but they both took different business models for their companies. EBay was built with a consumer to consumer model whereas Amazon opted for a business to consumer approach. Over time, both companies evolved into platforms where  businesses sell their goods to consumer and so the companies act as a third party sales platform where they earn a percentage through sales and advertisement. As secure payments, algorithms and cloud infrastructure developed it provided the two companies all the resources they needed to flourish as a business.
